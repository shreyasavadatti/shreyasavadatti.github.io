I'm a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University and a Quantum Research Assistant in CMU's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. I work on quantum error correction, fault tolerance, and certification: when a quantum device hands you an answer, what can you actually prove about it?

That question runs through most of what I do, from certified bounds on error-corrected memories to learning and inverting the noise of a trapped-ion gate. I hold my own work to the same standard: exact arithmetic behind the proofs, hardware runs reported with their failures, and code that reproduces every number in the paper.

Previously, I was a quantum computing intern at the U.S. Department of Energy's National Laboratory of the Rockies. I'm applying to PhD programs in quantum computing for Fall 2027.

Papers and preprints

Failure of Geometric Rényi Amortization Collapse Without Slot Assistance

Shreya Savadatti. Zenodo, 2026.

For ordinary quantum channels, the geometric Rényi divergence satisfies amortization collapse. I show that the analogous identity fails for superchannels when the inserted channels are restricted to the slot, for every 1 < α ≤ 2, which answers a problem posed in the QIQC Open Problem Zoo. Reference assistance restores the collapse.

Physicality, Relaxation Gaps, and Resource Allocation in Pauli-Limited Quantum Certification

Shreya Savadatti and P. Y. Zhang. 2026.

How well a gate can be certified from noisy Pauli measurements, and how much it helps to require the unknown channel to be physical. Two gates with the same stabilizer norm can behave differently. Includes computer-assisted proofs in interval and exact rational arithmetic.

Certified Separation Between Coherent and Adaptive Temporal Processing of Quantum-Error-Correction Readout

Shreya Savadatti. Manuscript, 2026.

Does keeping quantum information between rounds of error-correction readout help the final decision? In an idealized two-round benchmark, a receiver with a seven-dimensional memory has certified error below 24.21%, while every adaptive measure-and-feed-forward receiver stays above 25.14%.

Perpetual Fault Tolerance at Fixed Overhead for Contractive Quantum Filters and Recurrent Instruments

Shreya Savadatti. Manuscript, 2026.

Fault-tolerance bounds usually grow with the length of the computation. For a filter that forgets its past, I combine concatenated level reduction with trace-distance contraction to bound the error for all time at a concatenation level that does not depend on how long the stream runs.

Entanglement Budgets and Certified Energy Floors for Variational Quantum Circuits

Shreya Savadatti. Manuscript, 2026.

A VQE can fail because the circuit cannot represent the target or because training cannot find it. I bound the first failure before any training, from the entangling gates and total entangling angle a circuit carries across each cut.

Quantum Algorithms for Oscillatory Brinkman Flow: Preconditioning and Observable Estimation

Shreya Savadatti. Manuscript, 2026.

A preconditioner whose condition-number bound is independent of the grid, a block encoding, and query upper and lower bounds for estimating dissipation observables in porous-media flow. All scalings are stated under explicit access assumptions.

Learning and Inverting the Error of a Trapped-Ion ZZ Gate

Shreya Savadatti. Write-up, 2026. Supported by IonQ research credits.

A four-parameter error model for IonQ's native gate that can be learned without twirling, and a modified observable that removes the resulting bias. Validated in simulation so far, with hardware runs planned.

Finalist, Aqora quantum hackathon. Mitsubishi Chemical and AIST track.

Returns a fingerprint of the quantum state to the generative model instead of a single energy. It halves the entangling-gate count at no cost in exact energy.
